# Video Sales Letter (VSL) Scriptwriter

> Script high-converting Video Sales Letters (VSLs) and direct-response video presentations. Combines Conrado Adolpho’s proven 8-part sales letter framework with Jon Benson’s ugly-slide retention mechanics and universal objection elimination to drive massive video-to-purchase conversions.

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when you need to:

- **Write a complete Video Sales Letter (VSL)** from hook to closing CTA
- **Script direct-response video ads** or webinar sales pitches
- **Structure slide-by-slide presentations** with precise text-on-screen (TOS) cues and audio pacing
- **Neutralize prospect skepticism** and dismantle the 5 universal buying objections systematically
- **Pace video narration** (120-140 words per minute) with cognitive resets every 3-5 seconds
- **Design high-converting offer transitions** where the pitch feels like the natural next step

## Conrado Adolpho’s 8-Part VSL Framework

The script progresses through 8 psychological gates that lead the viewer to a state of buying readiness:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. HOOK & PATTERN INTERRUPT  Disrupt scrolling; reveal an unexpected truth (0-30s) |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2. EMPATHY & PROBLEM AGITATION Validate pain; prove deep understanding of struggles|
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3. ROOT CAUSE & COMMON ENEMY   Expose the real reason past attempts failed        |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 4. THE DISCOVERY & MECHANISM   The eureka moment and the proprietary new method   |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 5. THE OFFER REVEAL            Introduce the product as the vehicle for the method|
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 6. PROOF & SOCIAL VALIDATION   Case studies, verified screenshots, transformation |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 7. UNIVERSAL OBJECTION MATRIX  Systematically dismantle the 5 reasons people stall|
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 8. THE CLOSE & RISK REVERSAL   Ironclad guarantee + bonuses + urgent final CTA    |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## The Universal Objection Neutralization Matrix

Every VSL must confront and resolve these 5 objections before asking for the sale:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| OBJECTION           | UNDERLYING FEAR               | VSL COUNTER-STRATEGY        |
+---------------------+-------------------------------+-----------------------------+
| 1. "No Money"       | "I can't afford to waste this"| Cost of Inaction Comparison |
| 2. "No Time"        | "I won't have time to use it" | 15-Minute Daily Quickstart  |
| 3. "Won't Work Me"  | "My situation is unique/hard" | Specific ICP Case Studies   |
| 4. "Don't Trust You"| "Is this another internet scam"| Vulnerability + Real Metrics|
| 5. "Don't Need Now" | "I'll think about it later"   | Urgency, Scarcity & Fast Bonus|
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## Slide-by-Slide Visual Pacing & Retention Rules

The #1 killer of VSL conversion is cognitive fatigue. To maintain viewer retention:

1. **Ugly-Slide Simplicity (Jon Benson Method)**: Black text on clean white/off-white background. No distracting animated graphics or stock video montage during critical persuasion moments.
2. **Text-on-Screen (TOS) Synchronization**: Only 1 sentence or phrase per slide. As the voiceover reads the word, it appears on screen. This dual-channel sensory stimulation doubles message absorption.
3. **Pacing**: Target 130 words per minute. Change the visual slide every 3-5 seconds.
4. **Formatting Notation**: Use standardized production brackets in the script:
   - `[TOS: Exact words displayed on screen]`
   - `[AUDIO: Voiceover inflection, pause, or emphasis instructions]`
   - `[VISUAL: Product mockup, proof screenshot, or highlight box]`

## VSL Production & Review Checklist

```
RETENTION & PACING:
□ Hook delivers a high-stakes promise or pattern interrupt within the first 10 seconds
□ Pacing stays within 120-140 words per minute (tested with audio timer)
□ Slides change every 3-5 seconds; zero walls of text on screen

PERSUASION ARCHITECTURE:
□ Common enemy or root cause introduced before presenting the product
□ Unique mechanism named and explained simply with a visual metaphor
□ All 5 universal objections addressed systematically in the script
□ Social proof is undeniable and directly mirrors the primary customer persona

OFFER & CLOSE:
□ Value stack clearly lists every deliverable with perceived value breakdown
□ 100% money-back guarantee prominently featured with clear terms
□ Clear, explicit call to action instructing the viewer on the exact next click
□ Verified with geral-anti-slop: script feels human, conversational, and direct
```
