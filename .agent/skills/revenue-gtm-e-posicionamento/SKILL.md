# Go-To-Market (GTM) & Product Marketing Architect

> Orchestrate high-impact product launches, design new market categories, structure defensible positioning using the Value Proposition Canvas and STP frameworks, and de-risk commercial execution through pre-mortem diagnostics.

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when you need to:

- **Architect a Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy** for a new company, product line, or major feature
- **Define core positioning and messaging** using the Value Proposition Canvas (Osterwalder)
- **Execute Category Design** to reframe market boundaries and declare an obsolete status quo
- **Apply the STP Framework** (Segmentation, Targeting, Positioning) to prioritize beachhead markets
- **Run a Pre-Mortem analysis** to identify fatal operational, product, and market risks prior to launch
- **Orchestrate cross-functional launch tiers** (Tier 1 Flagship, Tier 2 Feature, Tier 3 Iteration)

## Strategic Frameworks Library

### 2. Category Design Architecture (Play Bigger)
Instead of competing in an overcrowded "red ocean" category, define a new problem space:
- **The Point of View (POV)**: Name the missing piece in the world. Declare the current way broken.
- **The Category Name**: Establish a simple, memorable noun phrase (e.g., *"Customer Data Platform"* instead of *"Database"*).
- **The Enemy**: Personify the villain (e.g., *"Disconnected silos"*, *"Spreadsheet chaos"*).
- **The Lightning Strike**: A coordinated event, launch, or campaign that forces the industry to acknowledge the new category.

*See [references/category-design.md](references/category-design.md) and [references/positioning-basics.md](references/positioning-basics.md).*

---

### 3. The Pre-Mortem Risk Mitigation Protocol (Gary Klein)
Before committing capital or resources to a launch:

1. **The Scenario**: *"Assume we launched 6 months ago. The product failed catastrophically. Adoption is near zero, churn is rampant, and pipeline missed target by 80%."*
2. **Individual Brainstorming**: Every stakeholder writes down every plausible reason for the disaster.
3. **Consolidation & Ranking**: Group risks by likelihood and impact (Product defects, messaging confusion, channel failure, pricing mismatch).
4. **Preventive Action Plan**: Assign concrete countermeasures and owners to the top 5 failure modes before launch day.

*See [pre-mortem.md](../revenue-modelos-mentais/references/pre-mortem.md) and [references/six-thinking-hats.md](../revenue-modelos-mentais/references/six-thinking-hats.md).*

## Strategic Mental Models & Marketing Psychology

Product marketers must leverage cognitive leverage to avoid blindspots, eliminate launch risks, and tap into buyer heuristics:

### 1. Inversion Thinking (Charlie Munger & Carl Jacobi)
Instead of asking *"How do we guarantee a successful launch?"*, ask *"How could this launch fail catastrophically?"*
- Identify all catastrophic failure paths: messaging confusion, server crashes, sales team unawareness, confusing pricing.
- Build systematic anti-failure gates before building the promotional calendar.
- *See [references/inversion.md](../revenue-modelos-mentais/references/inversion.md) and [references/inversion-exercise.md](../revenue-modelos-mentais/references/inversion-exercise.md).*

### 2. Marketing Psychology & Cognitive Biases
Buyers rely on mental shortcuts when evaluating new products:
- **Anchoring:** Establish reference pricing before presenting your core offer.
- **Loss Aversion:** Framing features around preventing losses converts 2x better than equivalent gains.
- **Social Proof & Bandwagon Effect:** Reduce uncertainty with peer adoption proofs, logo bars, and cohort case studies.
- **Cognitive Ease:** Simplify interface wording and value metrics to eliminate evaluation fatigue.
- *See [references/marketing-psychology.md](../revenue-psicologia/references/marketing-psychology.md) and [references/cognitive-biases.md](../revenue-psicologia/references/cognitive-biases.md).*

### 3. Eisenhower Prioritization Matrix for Launch Execution
Classify launch initiatives into 4 distinct quadrants:
- **Urgent & Important (Do Now):** Value proposition alignment, critical bug fixes, core landing page, payment gate.
- **Important but Not Urgent (Schedule):** Deep competitor intelligence, customer interview loops, partner co-marketing.
- **Urgent but Not Important (Delegate / Automate):** Social post scheduling, administrative tracking, routine status calls.
- **Neither (Eliminate):** Vanity redesigns, non-essential feature edge cases, secondary channel tests.
- *See [references/eisenhower-matrix.md](../revenue-modelos-mentais/references/eisenhower-matrix.md).*

## GTM Launch Readiness Checklist

```
POSITIONING & VALIDATION:
□ Value Proposition Canvas completed: clear 1-to-1 mapping between pains and pain relievers
□ STP Framework applied: specific beachhead target market isolated and quantified
□ Category POV articulated without relying on competitor comparisons
□ Pre-Mortem & Inversion conducted with cross-functional team; mitigations deployed

EXECUTION & ASSETS:
□ Core message approved: 1-sentence value proposition + 3-pillar proof narrative
□ Customer-facing assets produced: landing page, demo video, onboarding email flow
□ Sales enablement delivered: battlecards, objection handling guide, pitch deck
□ Success metrics defined: target signups, activation rate, pipeline influenced
```
