# Sonic Branding Strategist

> Sound drives up to 96% greater brand recall than visual elements alone. Strategic sonic branding transforms generic background audio into an unmistakable acoustic identity that triggers instant emotional connection, increases trust, and establishes cognitive stickiness across Reels, YouTube videos, podcasts, and digital products.

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when you need to:

- **Create a complete acoustic identity** for a new company or digital product
- **Design a memorable Sonic Logo (Audio Mnemonic)** (1 to 3 seconds, e.g., Netflix "Ta-dum", Intel, HBO)
- **Execute Step 8 (Audiovisual Activation) of the `brand-builder` protocol**
- **Establish audio guidelines for video production** (music selection, vocal EQ/pacing, sound effects)
- **Select signature voice profiles (Voice Design)** for AI narration, product explainers, and ads
- **Audit competitive audio landscapes** to avoid category clichés (e.g., generic corporate acoustic guitar or bland tech synth)

## 1. Acoustic Attribute Mapping

Translate core brand attributes into specific sonic parameters:

| Brand Attribute | Instrument Palette | Rhythm & Tempo | Harmony & Key | Sonic Texture |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Innovative & Cutting-Edge** | Analog synthesizers, granular glitch | 110–128 BPM, syncopated | Modal, minor 7ths | Crisp, high-frequency, wide stereo |
| **Trustworthy & Authoritative** | Warm acoustic piano, cellos, brass | 70–90 BPM, steady 4/4 | Major triads, resolved | Rich mid-lows, organic room reverb |
| **Playful & Energetic** | Plucked strings, mallet percussion | 120–135 BPM, bouncing | Bright major, pentatonic | Punchy, dry, dynamic accents |
| **Minimalist & Premium** | Sub-bass pulses, sparse glass pads | 60–80 BPM, spacious | Ambient, sustained drones | Deep sub, breathy air, isolated transients |

## 2. Core Acoustic Assets

### A. The Sonic Logo (Audio Mnemonic)
- **Duration:** 1.0 to 3.5 seconds max.
- **Role:** The audio signature played at the head/tail of videos, podcasts, software boots, and key conversion moments.
- **Structure:**
  - *Attack/Initiator:* High-arousal transient that grabs immediate attention.
  - *Body/Core Melody:* 2 to 4 distinct tonal notes expressing the emotional promise.
  - *Tail/Decay:* Harmonic resonance that lingers in memory.

### B. Signature Soundscape & Background Curation
- Establish rigid rules for background music in marketing assets:
  - Dialogue clearance: Strict ducking of frequencies between 500 Hz – 3 kHz so spoken voice always remains crisp.
  - No lyrical competition: Zero vocal tracks behind instructional or sales speech.

### C. Voice Design (Spoken Persona)
- **Cadence & Speed:** Measured conversational (130–150 words per minute) vs high-urgency fast pace (160–180 WPM).
- **Vocal Posture:** Resonant chest voice (authority) vs bright dynamic tone (accessibility).
- **Tone Spectrum:** Friendly mentor, analytical peer, or visionary challenger.

## 3. Touchpoint Deployment Matrix

| Channel / Touchpoint | Sonic Element | Duration | Primary Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reels / TikToks / Shorts** | Signature transition swoosh / intro cue | 0.5 – 1.0s | Scroll-stopping pattern interruption |
| **YouTube Long-Form** | Channel intro sonic logo + chapter transitions | 2.0 – 4.0s | Structural pacing and brand anchoring |
| **Product / App UX** | Micro-interactions (Success, Alert, Refresh) | 0.1 – 0.4s | Tactile feedback and dopamine reinforcement |
| **Webinars & Keynotes** | Walk-in soundscape + Walk-out outro theme | Loopable | Immersive atmosphere and status elevation |

## Implementation & Production Checklist

- [ ] Complete competitive audio audit (isolate competitor sound clichés)
- [ ] Define top 3 emotional acoustic targets (e.g., Confidence, Clarity, Forward Momentum)
- [ ] Build acoustic moodboard with 5 Spotify/YouTube reference tracks
- [ ] Compose 3 alternative sonic logo drafts (1.5s to 3s)
- [ ] Test sonic logo across iPhone speaker, laptop, and studio headphones
- [ ] Formulate Voice Design brief (timbre, tempo, mic proximity) for narrators and AI voices
- [ ] Export production-ready audio kit (.WAV 24-bit 48kHz and uncompressed .MP3)
