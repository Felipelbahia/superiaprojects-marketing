# Indicação — 55 experimentos B2B

Formato: **N. Título** — Hipótese. Métrica. Benchmark/fonte.

CAC do referral é tipicamente 50-80% menor que CAC pago. Em B2B, indicação é canal mais subexplorado vs C2C.

---

## Referral programs (cliente → cliente)

**1. Programa double-sided (indicador + indicado)** — Reward simétrico supera unilateral. % new MRR via referral. Programas bem desenhados em janelas curtas (≤10 dias) podem gerar dezenas de leads/contratos sem mídia paga.

**2. Cash reward por referral fechado** — $500-2.000 por deal fechado de referral. Referrals/cliente/ano. Standard B2B.

**3. Account credit (vs cash)** — Crédito na fatura em vez de dinheiro. Higher uptake vs cash. Modern B2B SaaS.

**4. Tiered referral rewards** — 1 ref = X, 5+ ref = Y, 10+ = Z (com upgrade). Referral velocity. Genius Referrals pattern.

**5. NPS-triggered referral request** — Pedir indicação só para promoters (NPS 9-10) no momento de pico. % promoters que indicam. Inflection 2025: NPS >50 correlaciona com 2-3x mais referrals.

**6. Referral ask no QBR** — Incluir "quem você indicaria?" como item de QBR. Referrals/QBR. Enterprise CS.

**7. Referral landing page com personalização** — URL custom com nome do indicador para referido. Conversion. Standard.

**8. Easy share buttons (Slack, email, LinkedIn)** — One-click share via canais B2B. Shares per customer. Modern referral UX.

**9. "Refer 3, get 1 free month"** — Bonus por volume mínimo. Referral velocity. Standard pattern.

**10. Charitable donation option** — Doar reward para charity escolhida. Higher participation vs cash em alguns segmentos. B2B CSR pattern.

## Partner / channel programs

**11. Partner program 20-30% revshare** — Partners (consultores, agências) com revshare lifetime. Pipeline via partner. Top 10% B2B SaaS têm 40%+ pipeline via partners.

**12. Tier de partner (Bronze/Silver/Gold)** — Tiers com benefícios crescentes. Partner-driven ARR. Standard channel.

**13. Co-selling com partner** — Sales conjunto, comp split. Win rate, deal size. Channel partnership.

**14. Partner-led implementation** — Implementação via partner com revshare de service. Customer retention pós-impl. Salesforce model.

**15. Solution provider directory** — Listar partners no website. Partner-driven leads. Modern B2B.

**16. Partner enablement program** — Treinamento + certificação para partners. Active partners, partner ARR. Microsoft Partner pattern.

**17. PartnerStack/Reveal/Crossbeam (partner CRM)** — Tooling para gerenciar partner pipeline. Partner attribution accuracy. Modern channel ops.

**18. Account mapping com partners** — Identify overlapping accounts entre seu CRM e partner. Joint pipeline. Crossbeam pattern.

## Customer advocacy

**19. G2/Capterra/TrustRadius review request via email** — Email automático request review pós-milestone. Reviews/mês. Navattic case (HubSpot): 25% via email vs 10.3% in-app.

**20. Review incentive (gift card $25)** — Reward simbólico. Review volume. Common B2B SaaS.

**21. Review-the-Renewal program** — Negociar review como parte do renewal package. Reviews/renewal. Enterprise.

**22. G2 Crowd top performer badge campaign** — Concentrar reviews em janela específica para conquistar badge. Reviews count, badge position. Modern review strategy.

**23. Customer reference program** — Banco de clientes que aceitam ser referência. Reference calls/quarter. Enterprise standard.

**24. Customer Advisory Board (CAB)** — 8-12 customers em board. Strategic input + advocacy. Enterprise CS.

**25. Speaking opportunities para customers** — Speaking spots em events da sua empresa para customers. Advocacy actions. Customer marketing.

**26. Customer awards program** — Annual awards para customers (innovation, scale, etc). Press, advocacy. Enterprise customer marketing.

**27. Customer champion network (private community)** — Slack/Discord exclusivo para advocate. NPS, retention, referrals. Reforge model.

## Case studies / Content

**28. Case study video co-criado** — Filmar com cliente, dar a ele direito de uso. Cases produced/quarter. Customer marketing.

**29. Case study quantitativo (números primeiro)** — Headline com número de impacto ($XM saved, X% growth). Case engagement. Standard.

**30. Vertical case study series** — 5+ cases do mesmo vertical para vertical playbook. Pipeline vertical-specific. Vertical SaaS pattern.

**31. Customer-led webinar** — Cliente apresenta sua jornada com seu produto. Pipeline pós-webinar. Customer marketing.

**32. Customer LinkedIn post com tag** — Cliente posta sobre uso, taga sua empresa. Brand mentions. Modern social proof.

**33. Customer podcast guests** — Convidar customers como guests no seu podcast. Episodes published, customer engagement. Modern customer marketing.

## Co-marketing / partnerships

**34. Co-marketing webinar** — Webinar conjunto com complementary B2B player. Leads gerados. Common B2B partnership.

**35. Co-authored research report** — Report conjunto com partner não-concorrente. Backlinks, leads. Modern content marketing.

**36. Joint case study (3-way)** — Você + Partner + Customer juntos no case. Distribution boost via 3 audiences. Enterprise pattern.

**37. Integration partner co-launch** — Launch conjunto de nova integração. Co-marketed leads. Tech partnership.

**38. Marketplace listing (Salesforce AppExchange, Slack App Directory, etc)** — Listing em marketplace de partner ecosystem. Marketplace-sourced leads. Standard SaaS distribution.

## Word-of-mouth / Viral

**39. Public sharing com branding ("Made with X")** — Compartilhamento público com branding leva a signups orgânicos. Direct signups via branded share. Loom/Calendly model.

**40. Customer-led product gallery** — Galeria pública de templates/setups criados por customers. Inbound via gallery. Notion/Webflow patterns.

## Influencer / Creator B2B

**41. B2B micro-influencer LinkedIn (5-50k followers)** — Sponsorship de post de creator no nicho. Inbound leads/post. La Growth Machine 2026.

**42. Founder-to-founder podcast appearance** — Founder participa de podcasts adjacentes do ICP. Pipeline pós-podcast. Foundera 2026.

**43. Newsletter sponsorship cruzado** — Sponsor de 2-3 newsletters do nicho com CTA único. CPL relativo. Newsletter sponsorship boom 2024-2026.

**44. Reddit AMA do founder** — AMA em subreddit vertical (r/SaaS, r/marketing, r/devops). Profile views, signups. Reddit B2B 2025.

**45. Twitter/X amplification (founder + executivos)** — Time interno amplifica posts do founder. Reach orgânico. La Growth Machine: 2.75x mais impressions vs company page.

## AI-powered advocacy

**46. AI customer advocacy detection** — ML identifica customers que postam organicamente sobre a marca. Outreach para amplificar. Advocacy actions. Modern customer marketing 2026.

**47. AI-generated case study draft** — AI rascunha case study a partir de QBR + product usage + transcripts. Cases produced/quarter. Modern customer marketing.

**48. AI personalized referral request** — Email gerado por AI baseado no momento ideal (NPS pico, milestone) + relacionamento. Referral conversion. Modern lifecycle 2026.

**49. AI partner attribution accuracy** — Crossbeam/PartnerStack + AI identifica overlapping accounts e atribui pipeline. Partner-driven ARR accuracy. Modern channel ops.

**50. AI co-marketing content generation** — AI gera assets para co-marketing (decks, posts, emails) automaticamente. Time-to-launch co-marketing. Modern partner marketing 2026.

## Channel & ecosystem deepening

**51. Marketplace listing optimization (AppExchange, Slack, HubSpot)** — Listing com video demo, reviews, integrations destacadas. Marketplace-sourced leads. Standard SaaS distribution.

**52. Tech alliance program (no revshare)** — Parceiros técnicos com integração mutual, sem comissão. Pipeline via integration. Modern tech partnerships.

**53. Customer hackathon / build day** — Evento onde customers constroem usando seu produto. Deep advocacy + cases públicos. Notion/Webflow.

**54. Customer reference rotation system** — Banco rotativo de customers que aceitam reference calls. Reference-driven win rate. Enterprise standard.

**55. NPS triggered LinkedIn share request** — Promoter (NPS 9-10) recebe pedido de share específico (não review). Branded LinkedIn shares. Modern advocacy 2026.

---

**Total nesta categoria: 55 experimentos.**

Notas:
- Em B2B, indicação é canal mais barato (CAC -50 a -80% vs paid).
- Top performers B2B SaaS têm 25-40% do pipeline via referral + partner combinados.
- Referral funciona melhor quando pedido em momento de pico de NPS (não aleatório).
- Partner programs precisam de 12-18 meses para maturar — não são quick wins.
