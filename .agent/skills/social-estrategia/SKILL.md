# Social Media Strategist & Organic Growth Architect

> Plan, write, and execute platform-native social media strategies across LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and Instagram. Leverage algorithmic mechanics (dwell time, bookmarks, comment velocity) and authentic engagement loops to build loyal audiences and drive pipeline.

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when you need to:

- **Build an editorial social media calendar** aligned with company pillars and GTM launches
- **Draft platform-native posts** optimized for specific algorithms (LinkedIn text/documents, X threads, Instagram carousels)
- **Engineer viral hooks** and opening lines that stop feed-scrolling
- **Analyze social metrics and sentiment** using structured social listening frameworks
- **Design comment-to-DM conversion funnels** and outbound engagement routines
- **Source and curate User-Generated Content (UGC)** or vet micro-influencer partnerships

## Platform-Specific Algorithmic Mechanics

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PLATFORM    | PRIMARY RANKING SIGNAL    | HIGHEST VALUE FORMAT  | HOOK WINDOW     |
+-------------+---------------------------+-----------------------+-----------------+
| LinkedIn    | Dwell Time + Discussion   | Document (PDF) / Text | First 2 lines   |
| X (Twitter) | Bookmarks + Replies       | Multi-tweet Thread    | Opening Tweet   |
| Instagram   | Saves + Shares            | 4:5 Carousel / Reel   | Slide 1 (1.5s)  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 1. LinkedIn Architecture
- **The "See More" Click**: The opening 2 lines (before the fold) determine whether the reader expands the post. Never waste this on greetings or context; lead with a contrarian insight, surprising data point, or compelling question.
- **Dwell Time Optimization**: Multi-slide PDF documents and clean formatting with whitespace increase the time spent on the post, signaling high value to the algorithm.
- **Discussion Velocity**: Ask an open-ended, non-generic question at the end to trigger replies in the first 60 minutes.

### 2. X (Twitter) Architecture
- **Hook Tweet Formula**: `[Specific Outcome or Contrarian Claim] + [Proof / Timeframe] + [Curiosity Gap]`.
- **Bookmark Triggers**: Checklists, curated resource lists, and step-by-step blueprints drive bookmarks, the single heaviest weighted signal in X's recommendation algorithm.
- **Thread Formatting**: Each tweet in a thread must stand alone as a valuable insight while bridging to the next tweet.

## The 4 Core Social Content Pillars

Every balanced social feed maintains a 40/30/20/10 ratio across these pillars:

1. **Tactical & Educational (40%)**: Actionable step-by-step how-tos, checklists, and breakdown frameworks that provide immediate utility.
2. **Point of View & Contrarian (30%)**: Strong industry stances, dismantling obsolete conventional wisdom, and founder perspective.
3. **Case Studies & Proof (20%)**: Teardowns of successful campaigns, client transformations, or internal company experiments.
4. **Behind-the-Scenes & Culture (10%)**: Transparent learnings from failures, team rituals, and raw building-in-public reflections.

*See [references/social-content.md](references/social-content.md) and [references/social-media-playbook.md](references/social-media-playbook.md).*

## Social Post Quality & Velocity Checklist

Before scheduling or publishing any social content:

```
HOOK & FORMATTING:
□ Hook contains a clear information gap or tension (no corporate throat-clearing)
□ Native formatting respected (no external link in the primary body if platform penalizes it)
□ Ample whitespace used; mobile scannability verified (no paragraphs longer than 2 lines)

RETENTION & ENGAGEMENT:
□ Post delivers on the exact promise made in the hook (no bait-and-switch)
□ Specific CTA included: save/bookmark, reply with opinion, or comment for a resource
□ Tone is conversational, sharp, and confident; zero AI slop ("In today's fast-paced world")

DISTRIBUTION:
□ Relevant topic tags or hashtags applied conservatively (3-5 max, no spam blocks)
□ Follow-up engagement plan ready: respond to all comments within the first 60 minutes
```
