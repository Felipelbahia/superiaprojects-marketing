# SEO Content Writer

> Write search-optimized content that ranks on Google using proven frameworks from Ahrefs, Moz, and Google's E-E-A-T guidelines—create helpful, people-first content that satisfies both search engines, generative engines, and human readers.

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when you need to:

- **Write blog posts** optimized for search engines and organic discoverability
- **Create pillar content & topic clusters** for definitive topical authority
- **Optimize existing content** for better rankings and fresh search intent
- **Write product, solution, or service pages** that convert search traffic
- **Create how-to guides, tutorials, and comparison articles**
- **Develop comprehensive content briefs** for writers or AI generation pipelines
- **Audit and score content** for E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)
- **Implement Programmatic SEO (pSEO)** architectures at scale

## Detailed Execution Guidelines

### Phase 1: Keyword Research & Intent Taxonomy

Before writing, ensure there is verified search demand and map the exact searcher intent.

1. **Keyword Qualification**:
   - **Search volume**: Monthly searches (high-volume head terms vs high-converting long-tail terms).
   - **Keyword difficulty (KD)**: Can your domain realistically rank based on current authority?
   - **Business relevance**: Does ranking for this term drive qualified pipeline or product interest?
   - **Traffic potential**: Total topic traffic including related LSI and long-tail variants.

2. **The 4 Intent Categories**:
   - **Informational**: User wants to learn (*"how to set up utm parameters"*, *"what is geo optimization"*). Formats: Step-by-step guides, explainer articles, tutorials.
   - **Commercial Investigation**: User is comparing options before purchase (*"best seo tools 2026"*, *"ahrefs vs semrush"*). Formats: Comparison tables, pros/cons, buyer guides.
   - **Transactional**: User wants to act (*"buy qr generator license"*, *"signup plinq"*). Formats: Product pages, pricing calculators.
   - **Navigational**: User is looking for a specific brand or URL (*"plinq login"*).


---

### Phase 2: Topic Clusters & Semantic Architecture

Do not publish standalone articles. Connect content into structured topic clusters to build topical authority:

- **Pillar Page**: Broad, comprehensive guide targeting high-volume head keywords (e.g., *"Ultimate Guide to Marketing Analytics"*).
- **Cluster Spokes**: Dedicated in-depth articles targeting specific subtopics (e.g., *"How to Build UTM URLs"*, *"GA4 Channel Grouping"*, *"Attribution Models Comparison"*).
- **Bidirectional Linking**: Every cluster spoke must link up to the pillar page, and the pillar page must link down to every spoke.


---

### Phase 3: Content Brief & Structure

Prepare a standardized content brief before drafting:

1. **Working Title** (using high-CTR formulas)
2. **Primary & Secondary Keywords** (with volume and KD)
3. **Search Intent & Dominant SERP Format**
4. **Unique Value Proposition / Information Gain Angle**
5. **Detailed H2/H3 Outline** with estimated word counts per section
6. **Key Questions to Answer** (derived from "People Also Ask" and competitor gaps)
7. **Internal & External Link Targets**

*Use the templates in [references/seo-content-brief.md](references/seo-content-brief.md) and [references/content-structure-templates.md](references/content-structure-templates.md).*

---

### Phase 4: E-E-A-T & Information Gain

Google rewards content that offers **Information Gain**—novel facts, unique perspectives, or proprietary data not found in existing search results.

1. **Experience (First-hand Proof)**:
   - Include real screenshots, step-by-step walk-throughs, and personal testing observations.
   - Use phrasing like *"In our tests of 45 campaigns, we found..."* instead of generic platitudes.
2. **Expertise (Depth & Accuracy)**:
   - Provide precise definitions, formulas, and edge cases.
   - Add expert author bios with verifiable credentials.
3. **Authoritativeness (Recognition & Citations)**:
   - Link out to authoritative industry sources, official documentation, or original research.
   - Embed quotable soundbites with clear attribution.
4. **Trustworthiness (Transparency & Rigor)**:
   - Provide clear dates, changelogs, methodology notes, and honest limitations.

*Consult [seo-geo/references/quotable-content-examples.md](../seo-geo/references/quotable-content-examples.md) for phrasing that earns organic citations and AI mentions.*

---

### Phase 5: High-Impact Drafting Patterns

Structure the content for effortless reading and skimmability:

- **The Hook (First 100 Words)**: Immediately validate the reader's problem, confirm they are in the right place, and state what they will achieve.
- **Direct Answer First (BLUF - Bottom Line Up Front)**: Answer the core target query directly under the first H2 in 2-3 clear sentences. This maximizes featured snippet and AI Overview eligibility.
- **Skimmable Formatting**:
  - Keep paragraphs to 2-4 sentences max.
  - Use bulleted and numbered lists for sequences.
  - Use comparative markdown tables for multidimensional data.
  - Bold key terms and takeaways.
- **Visual Pauses**: Add diagrams, callout blocks, or visual examples every 300-400 words.

*See [references/content-patterns.md](references/content-patterns.md) and [references/write-content.md](references/write-content.md).*

---

### Phase 6: High-CTR Headlines & Title Formulas

Headlines make or break organic CTR:

1. **Title Tag Structure**: `Primary Keyword - Secondary Hook | Brand` (keep under 60 characters).
2. **Proven CTR Formula Types**:
   - **The Complete Guide**: *"UTM Tracking: The Complete Guide for 2026 Marketers"*
   - **The Actionable How-To**: *"How to Build a Topic Cluster in 5 Steps (With Templates)"*
   - **The Data/Outcome Hook**: *"We Analyzed 1,000 QR Codes: Here Is What Drives Scans"*
   - **The Comparison**: *"Ahrefs vs Semrush: 2026 In-Depth Comparison for Agencies"*
   - **The Negative/Mistake Hook**: *"7 UTM Tagging Mistakes Killing Your Attribution Data"*

*Consult [references/title-formulas.md](references/title-formulas.md) for 50+ battle-tested headline frameworks.*

---

### Phase 7: Programmatic SEO (pSEO) Considerations

When scaling content across hundreds of templated landing pages (e.g., location pages, tool integrations, comparison matrices):

- Ensure programmatic templates deliver **unique value per page** (not just search-and-replace variables).
- Enforce minimum word count and dynamic data points per generated page.
- Audit indexation health and avoid thin-content penalties.

## E-E-A-T Pre-Publish Checklist

Before publishing, verify each item:

```markdown
### E-E-A-T Quality Audit Checklist

EXPERIENCE:
□ Includes first-hand insights, tests, or original data
□ Visual evidence present (original screenshots, diagrams, charts)
□ Describes real workflows rather than theoretical advice

EXPERTISE:
□ Author bio with credentials and domain expertise is visible
□ Accurate technical terminology used without fluff or AI clichés
□ Thoroughly covers subtopics and answers related search queries

AUTHORITATIVENESS:
□ Cites primary data, official specs, or recognized industry studies
□ In-content quotes or expert insights included
□ Strong internal links to and from related cluster content

TRUSTWORTHINESS:
□ Factual claims verified; publication/update date displayed
□ Neutral, balanced evaluation (discloses caveats or affiliate relationships)
□ Clear CTAs aligned with search intent (no bait-and-switch)
```
