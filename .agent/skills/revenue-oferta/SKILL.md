# Grand Slam Offer Architect ($100M Offers)

> Create offers so compelling, unique, and value-stacked that prospects feel stupid saying no. Based on Alex Hormozi’s proven framework, this skill guides value equation optimization, bonus stacking, risk reversal engineering, and high-converting offer naming.

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when you need to:

- **Create an irresistible core offer** for a product, service, SaaS, agency, or course
- **Differentiate from competitors** to escape price wars and commoditization
- **Maximize the 4 variables of the Value Equation** to charge premium prices
- **Structure high-perceived-value bonus stacks** that overcome specific purchase hesitations
- **Design unbreakable guarantees and risk reversals** (conditional, unconditional, anti-guarantees)
- **Apply the M-A-G-I-C naming formula** to make the offer instantly memorable and clear

## Alex Hormozi’s Value Equation

Perceived value is governed by four distinct variables:

$$\text{Value} = \frac{\text{Dream Outcome} \times \text{Perceived Likelihood of Achievement}}{\text{Time Delay} \times \text{Effort \& Sacrifice}}$$

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| VARIABLE                      | OBJECTIVE             | TACTICAL IMPLEMENTATION   |
+-------------------------------+-----------------------+---------------------------+
| 1. Dream Outcome (Top)        | Maximize aspiration   | Connect to status & relief|
| 2. Likelihood of Success (Top)| Maximize certainty    | Social proof, guarantees  |
| 3. Time Delay (Bottom)        | Minimize to zero      | Immediate fast-action wins|
| 4. Effort & Sacrifice (Bottom)| Minimize friction     | "Done-for-you", templates |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

To make an offer 100x more valuable, **increase the top of the equation** and **decrease the bottom to near zero**.

## The 5-Step Offer Creation Sequence

1. **Identify the Dream Outcome**: What does the buyer actually want (status, freedom, revenue, relief)?
2. **List Every Obstacle**: Brainstorm every single friction point, fear, and technical hurdle the buyer faces before, during, and after purchase.
3. **Turn Obstacles into Solutions**: Reframe every obstacle into a specific solution.
4. **Determine the Delivery Vehicle**: How will you deliver the solution (1-on-1, small group, DIY templates, done-with-you, software)?
5. **Trim and Stack**: Cut low-value, high-effort items; keep high-value, low-cost-to-deliver assets (templates, checklists, scripts, software access).

## Enhancing the Offer: Scarcity, Urgency, Bonuses & Guarantees

### 1. Scarcity & Urgency (Ethical Levers)
- **Physical Scarcity**: Real limits on batch sizes or cohort seats (*"Only 20 onboarding slots this month"*).
- **Temporal Urgency**: Hard deadlines linked to real operational cycles (*"Closes Friday midnight before sprint kick-off"*).

### 2. Bonus Stacking Psychology
Every bonus must **destroy a specific objection** to buying the core offer:
- Objection: *"I don't have time to write the copy."* -> Bonus: *"Pre-written swipe files & prompt templates."*
- Objection: *"My team won't know how to set this up."* -> Bonus: *"15-minute white-glove onboarding session."*
- Rule: Always state the individual retail value of each bonus before revealing the stacked total.

*See [references/grand-slam-offers.md](references/grand-slam-offers.md) (stacking, guarantees, scarcity) and [references/objection-library.md](references/objection-library.md).*

### 3. Risk Reversal (Guarantees)
- **Unconditional 30-Day Money-Back**: Zero questions asked. Removes 100% of consumer friction.
- **Conditional / Performance-Based**: *"If you complete the 4 action steps and don't see X outcome in 60 days, we work with you for free until you do."*
- **Anti-Guarantee**: All sales final; explicitly weeds out non-committal tire-kickers for bespoke services.

## The M-A-G-I-C Offer Naming Formula

Make your offer title irresistible by combining these 5 components:

1. **M — Magnetic Reason**: Why are you making this offer? (e.g., *"Free 14-Day Challenge"*, *"VIP Growth Sprint"*).
2. **A — Avatar**: Who is this specifically for? (e.g., *"for B2B SaaS Founders"*).
3. **G — Goal**: What is the dream outcome? (e.g., *"to Add $10k MRR"*).
4. **I — Interval**: In what timeframe? (e.g., *"in 45 Days"*).
5. **C — Container**: What is the delivery vehicle? (e.g., *"Masterclass"*, *"System"*, *"Sprint"*).

*Example*: *"The 6-Week B2B Pipeline Sprint: How Early-Stage Founders Close 5 Enterprise Deals in 45 Days."*

## Grand Slam Offer Review Checklist

```
VALUE EQUATION RIGOR:
□ Dream outcome clearly articulates an upgrade in customer status or peace of mind
□ Fast-action immediate win delivered on Day 1 to collapse time delay
□ "Done-with-you" or template components included to slash customer effort

BONUS & GUARANTEE STACK:
□ Every included bonus addresses a specific reason why the prospect might fail or hesitate
□ Bonuses have realistic, stated retail value anchors
□ Guarantee is prominently displayed with explicit conditions or zero-friction terms
□ Top 5 universal objections systematically addressed in the offer presentation

OFFER NAMING & COPY:
□ M-A-G-I-C formula applied to the offer headline
□ Scarcity or urgency is grounded in a believable operational constraint
□ Checked with geral-anti-slop: copy is bold, direct, and zero generic marketing fluff
```
